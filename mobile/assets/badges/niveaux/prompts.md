# Série de badges de niveaux — style flat

Génération : outil intégré imagegen. Niveau 1 : prototype bois validé, conservé tel quel. Niveaux 2 à 10 : générations distinctes, avec ce prototype comme référence de style uniquement. Tous les PNG conservent leur transparence.

## Niveau 2 — Pierre

Fichier : `02-pierre.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: ONE standalone game rank badge for a fitness app, RGBA PNG, genuine transparent background.
The supplied wooden level-1 badge is a STYLE GUIDE ONLY: keep its clean flat mobile-game illustration, bold dark outlines, broad color facets and shallow graphic bevels. DO NOT copy its wooden shield silhouette, turquoise chevron, layout or palette. This rank must have its own clearly distinct design, not be a recolor of level 1.
Style: bold readable 2D game icon, simplified flat-color illustration, crisp edges, a limited palette, 2–3 adjacent shades per material, shallow depth expressed through flat bevel bands. Material communicated through shape and color. No photorealistic surface, no complex 3D lighting, no realistic microtexture, no glossy plastic. Premium crafted game asset, legible at 80–100 pixels.
Composition: square canvas, front-facing, balanced near-symmetry, one cohesive centered badge. Leave 10% transparent padding on ALL sides, fully uncropped, no scene, no interface. The rank numeral dominates the center and remains instantly readable.
Transparency: TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA outside the badge. No black, white, colored or gradient backdrop, no painted checkerboard. No floor, drop shadow, glow cloud, smoke or particles around the silhouette. Graphic highlights remain inside the badge.
Text: ONLY the specified Arabic numeral, once. No letters, Roman numerals, banner labels, logo or watermark.

SPECIFIC RANK DESIGN:
Rank 2: an understated rugged stone rank, slightly stronger than the wooden starter. Distinct silhouette: a broad compact OCTAGONAL stone tablet, clipped corners, no pointed shield bottom. Thick layered slate-grey stone rim, blue-grey recessed inner stone, two or three broad carved cracks confined to the border. A small moss-green diamond inset at the very bottom. Central numeral "2" carved in light warm limestone with charcoal outline and simple flat bevel. Heavy solid masonry feel with clean flat surfaces; no crown, wings or laurel. Palette charcoal, cool grey, ivory and restrained moss green.
```
